# Hithub — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-09-13

This Privacy Policy explains how Hithub ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information from users of the App. By using Hithub you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

## Information We Collect
- Usage Data: anonymous analytics, feature usage, crash reports, timestamps.
- Device Information: device model, OS version, app version, locale.
- Optional Personal Data: only if you provide it (e.g., email address for account or support).
- No sensitive personal information (e.g., biometric, health, financial) is collected by default.

## How We Use Information
- Provide and maintain the App.
- Improve features and user experience through analytics and crash reports.
- Respond to support requests if you contact us.
- Comply with legal obligations.

## Data Sharing and Third Parties
- We do not sell personal data.
- We may share aggregated or anonymized data that does not identify you.
- We use third-party services for analytics and crash reporting (for example: Firebase, Google Play services). These providers have their own privacy policies.

## Data Retention
- We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the service and for legitimate business purposes (e.g., analytics, troubleshooting), or as required by law.

## Security
- We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data.
- No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure; absolute security cannot be guaranteed.
